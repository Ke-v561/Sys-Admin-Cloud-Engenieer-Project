# Portfolio guide

Publish evidence that proves skills, not just attendance.

Good public artifacts:
- Sanitized network and identity diagrams.
- Lab reports with objective, implementation, validation, failure, and resolution.
- PowerShell and shell scripts with comments and safe defaults.
- Terraform/Bicep modules and deployment instructions.
- Architecture decision records and runbooks.
- Screenshots with names, IDs, IPs, and tenant details redacted.

Never publish credentials, tokens, private keys, `.tfstate`, real customer/employer data, public IPs tied to your home, or unredacted logs.

For each project, include: problem, requirements, design, implementation, security, validation, cost controls, failure handling, cleanup, and lessons learned.
